--- 1. Notes for the reviewer (English, paste as is) ----------------------------------------------------------------

Drushe is a peer-tutoring app in Hebrew: teenagers (14-18) teach younger children (9-18) under parental supervision.
Every account is reviewed before it is active:
- A student registration requires the parent to click a consent link emailed to the parent, and then an administrator
  approves the account.
- A teacher registration additionally requires an ID photo and the same parent consent.
Because of this, a fresh sign-up cannot be used during review. Please use these pre-approved accounts:

   Student account:  <EMAIL>   password: <PASSWORD>
   Teacher account:  <EMAIL>   password: <PASSWORD>

(The owner must create both accounts, complete the parent consent for each, and approve them in the admin panel
BEFORE submitting. Keep the credentials out of the repository.)

Child safety and user-generated content (Guideline 1.2):
- Users can report any profile, post, question, group or chat from the app (🚨 button) and block any user
  (Profile → Blocked users). Reports go to the administrator, who handles them with a documented procedure.
- A teacher can only write to a child after the child started the conversation. Contact details (links, phone
  numbers, @handles) are rejected in public text by the app and by the database rules. Chats are scanned for
  attempts to move the conversation off the app and are flagged to the administrator.
- Parents receive an email when a new conversation starts, and can log in with the child's credentials to see activity.

Account deletion (5.1.1(v)): Profile → "Delete my account" removes the account and its data.
Payments: lessons are agreed and paid directly between the student's family and the teacher; the app does not process,
display or record any payment for a lesson. The iOS app contains no purchase flow and no external-payment links.
Push notifications are optional and used only for new messages and safety reports.

--- 2. App Privacy ("nutrition label") — what to declare -------------------------------------------------------------

Tracking: NO. No data is used to track users across other companies' apps or websites. No advertising SDKs.

Data collected and linked to the user (all for App Functionality; none used for tracking):
  - Contact info: name, email address (the parent's email for minors), phone number is NOT collected
  - User content: photos (profile photo, optional story, teacher ID photo), other user content (posts, messages,
    reviews, group and lesson details)
  - Identifiers: user ID; device push token (only if notifications are allowed)
  - Location: coarse/precise location only if the user taps "teachers near me" (optional, used for search, not stored
    with the profile unless the teacher saves a city)
  - Usage data: product interaction (analytics) — collected only AFTER parental consent / account verification
  - Diagnostics: crash/error logs (clientErrors)
  - Sensitive/other: government ID number and ID photo of a minor TEACHER and of the parent (consent / age
    verification) — declare under "Other data"; visible to the administrator only, deleted with the account.

Data not collected: health, financial info (lesson payments never touch the app), contacts, browsing history,
search history, purchases.
